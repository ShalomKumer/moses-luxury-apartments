## Accessibility of the site

We work to make the site accessible to everyone, including people who use a screen reader, magnification or only a keyboard. The site is built to the WCAG 2.1 guidelines at level AA, on which the Israeli standard SI 5568 is based.

What was done on the site:

- The whole site can be used from a keyboard, including the photo gallery and the guest selector.
- Every photo has a text description.
- The colours were checked for sufficient contrast.
- A "Skip to the main content" link is at the top of every page.
- The site is available in Hebrew and in English, and each page declares its language and its writing direction.
